➕ Addition Concept

What is Addition?

Addition means putting things together to make a bigger group! The plus sign + means "add".

🍎 Interactive Example: 3 + 2 = 5

➖ Subtraction Concept

What is Subtraction?

Subtraction means taking away items from a group to see how many are left! The minus sign - means "take away".

🎈 Interactive Example: Pop Balloons to Subtract!

Start with 5 balloons. Click balloons to POP them!

Start: 5 - Popped: 0 = Left: 5
